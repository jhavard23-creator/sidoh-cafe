WHO WE ARE

A Passion for Great Coffee

At SIDÓH CAFE, we believe great coffee brings people together. A coffee shop should be more than a place to grab a drink. It should be a welcoming place where people can relax, enjoy their favorite coffee, and spend time with friends and family.

We appreciate the simple pleasures of a good cup of coffee, a friendly atmosphere, and the connections that happen around the table.

SHARING OUR LOVE OF COFFEE

Introducing Family Friend Coffee

Our passion for coffee goes beyond our own café. We also want to introduce Family Friend Coffee beans to other coffee shops and cafés.

We believe great coffee can create connections between local businesses and their communities. Our goal is to help more cafés discover Family Friend Coffee and explore opportunities to offer these beans to their customers.

OUR MISSION

Bringing Great Coffee to Everyone

Our mission is to celebrate great coffee, create welcoming experiences, and build connections with other coffee shops. Through SIDÓH CAFE and Family Friend Coffee, we hope to share our love of coffee with more people and businesses.
